# Design brief — PolicyDesk

**Name:** PolicyDesk
**Category:** Synthetic policy-decision sandbox.
**User:** A reviewer testing how a fictionally complete customer case moves from ticket to rule decision to customer reply.
**Job:** Choose a scenario, edit the fictional facts, read the deterministic decision and its evidence, then inspect the simulated approval boundary.
**Artifact:** The ticket, editable order facts, policy clauses, decision, reply and decision trail.
**Evidence:** Deterministic rule output, cited clauses, simulated approval, synthetic labels and the explicit public/OpenAI boundary.
**Drift to refuse:** generic civic blue, tiny labels, mouse-only scenario rail, color-only decisions, marketing hero.
**Register:** Product / decision sandbox.
